how it works

Three moves.

01

Launch

Pick any anon on X, name the coin, pick the stock it pays in. Launch it on Pons with the ALT description and the ALT vault as fee recipient.

02

Trade

Every trade pays 1%. Half waits in the anon's vault. A fifth pays the launcher. The rest is Pons. Swept every hour, posted with the transaction.

03

Reveal

Only the account can claim its vault — by posting a code from the account. We read it from X. The only reason to unmask is the money.

I.Every alt is a coin. Anyone can launch one on any anon.
II.Every trade pays 1%: half waits for the anon, a fifth pays the launcher, the rest is Pons.
III.Only the anon can claim. A post with the code, verified from X.
IV.Market cap per follower, not market cap. Read from the chain and from X, never typed in.
Does the anon have to agree?

No. Anyone can launch a coin on any public account. The account is the pair, not a partner. What the anon gets is a vault that only they can open.

What if the anon never claims?

The vault keeps filling. It is swept to a holding wallet every hour and the balance per alt is shown on its card and in the claims ledger. It waits.

How do you know it's really them?

The code is derived from the handle and the wallet. The account posts it. We read the post from X's public syndication endpoint and check the author and the code. No login, no keys.

Where does the 1% go?

Pons pays 0.7% of every trade to the fee recipient and keeps 0.3%. The recipient for every alt is the ALT vault wallet, which pays 0.2% to the launcher and holds 0.5% for the anon. The ledger is public.

What is "per follower"?

Market cap divided by the account's followers, read live. A $100K coin on a 10K-follower anon is $10 a follower. It's the number to compare alts by.

Is the split on chain?

Not yet. The vault wallet does the split by hand every hour and posts every transaction. The vault contract that does it automatically is the next thing we ship.
